# Authoring and governance

**Audience:** a contributor proposing or revising a profile. **Canonical sources:** [`spec/profiles.md`](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/dpp/blob/main/spec/profiles.md) §2, §4, [`GOVERNANCE.md`](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/dpp/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) (versioning), [`packages/dpp-profiles/README.md`](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/dpp/blob/main/packages/dpp-profiles/README.md) (working on it).

## The lifecycle of a profile

1. **Propose.** A manifest under the manifest schema (version 2 for a new profile), naming its use case, the baseline and roles it requires, its dependencies, the combinations it is incompatible with, its sources with their licences and retrieval state, and every field with the properties the framework requires. [Proposing a profile](../contribute/profiles.md) is the process.
2. **Generate.** `npm run build -w @bsv/dpp-profiles` regenerates every payload schema, consumer document and mapping inventory from the manifests and refuses drift from `frozen.json`.
3. **Freeze.** `npm run refreeze` writes the current digests into the manifests and `frozen.json`; the diff is the review, and a reviewed change to an existing version's generated output is the only legitimate use.
4. **Publish.** The profile joins the package and the ledger with its own rows and, where a product claim exists, its own withheld claim until assessed.
5. **Succeed.** A changed meaning, a corrected mandatory field or an incompatible rule is a new version carrying a migration outcome per predecessor field. The predecessor stays served and is never edited; the cutover that marks it superseded is a later reviewed re-freeze.

## What a profile may and may not do

It may select fields, units, evidence and access rules, and name the conditions under which an operation maps to an external event. It may not change the record, signature, history or anchoring rules, add a switch where one named tested choice would do, or read a state issued under a predecessor under its own rules. It must keep obligation apart from legal basis, mark what needs review as needing review, and name its sources with their licences; a licensed text that could not be read is recorded as unassessed, and no clause is invented from a catalogue summary.

## Tests a profile ships with

Manifest validity, uniqueness of identifiers, the frozen digests, deterministic generation, payload validation against the generated schemas with positive and refusal cases, and the mapping inventory. A profile with a demonstration lifecycle ships it as a fixture generated by a test, like the battery one.
