# Passport projections

**Profile:** `passport-projection@1`. **Canonical sources:** [`spec/passport-projections.md`](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/dpp/blob/main/spec/passport-projections.md), [`contracts/passport-source.schema.json`](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/dpp/blob/main/contracts/passport-source.schema.json), [`contracts/passport-projection.schema.json`](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/dpp/blob/main/contracts/passport-projection.schema.json), [`contracts/profile-evidence.schema.json`](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/dpp/blob/main/contracts/profile-evidence.schema.json), [`fixtures/vectors/dpp/interoperability/projection/`](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/dpp/blob/main/fixtures/vectors/dpp/interoperability/projection/README.md). **Example:** `node examples/project-passport.mjs`.

A product's values come from more than one place: a model revision, a batch revision, an item revision, measurements over time, corrections. A projection derives one passport's values from pinned revisions of those sources under a named policy, and the same inputs give the same digest whatever the arrival order. Source revisions are append-only with a compare-and-swap current pointer; relationships are typed (instance of a model, member of a batch, component of, successor of); the projection envelope's digest commits to the pinned sources, the policy, the cutoff and the disclosure and excludes its own identity.

The precedence rules are the profile's: a declared override wins, an undeclared conflict is reported with both sources, an exact-scope measurement is selected by observation time, corrections replay to one result, zero and false are preserved, availability is reported apart from applicability, traversal is bounded and a cycle or a limit is a finding. The shared shapes for claim evidence, certification, measurement, related document, language and date precision are the contract's, and manifest version 2 profiles use them.

The vectors cover deterministic projections from versioned sources, out-of-order measurements and correction replay reaching the same digest, cycles and limits as findings, withheld restricted fields, month-precision dates with a purchase-based warranty, fraction against percent with an explicit basis, zero and false preserved, and language fallback (5 positive, 3 refusal), plus packaging cases (1 and 2). A projection is a data operation; it writes no token state and asserts nothing a signature did not.
