# Passport projection vectors

`v1.json` pins `passport-projection@1` ([`../../../../../spec/passport-projections.md`](../../../../../spec/passport-projections.md) §4, [`../../../../../contracts/passport-projection.schema.json`](../../../../../contracts/passport-projection.schema.json)) in the BSV stack's conformance vector format. The file is generated by `packages/dpp-profiles/test/projections.test.ts` from the scenario in `test/projection-fixture.ts` and held identical to it; `REGENERATE_FIXTURES=1` rewrites it. Nothing in it describes a product that exists: the model is under the GS1 demonstration prefix 952, the hosts are `example.org`, and the profile is the synthetic version 2 manifest that exists only to exercise the rules.

The profile is the same for every vector and is carried once under the top-level `shared.profile`; a vector's `input.profile` is `{ "$shared": "profile" }`, which a runner replaces with that manifest before deriving. Every other input is inline: the source revisions with their payloads and digests, the relationships, the measurements, the policy, the cutoff, the audience and the locale.

Each vector's `expected` carries the `projectionDigest` and `projectionId` the derivation yields, `replayedDigest` (the digest from the same inputs in a shuffled arrival order, equal by contract), the `values`, `fieldResults`, `sourceRefs` and `relationshipRefs`, the validation finding codes, and whether the derivation was complete. An implementation in another language reproduces the digest by building the commitment body the specification defines, canonicalising it under RFC 8785 and hashing it with SHA-256.

| Vector | What it proves |
|---|---|
| `item-public` | The pinned model revision is read rather than the newest; a declared batch override applies; an undeclared batch value is a conflict with both sources; the dynamic cycle count is selected; the owner and legitimate-interest tiers are withheld; the batch month keeps month precision; the language is selected exactly; zero and false survive; a fraction with a stated basis converts explicitly. |
| `item-legitimate` | The state of health is the latest accepted observation with invalid and revoked readings set aside, and the legitimate-interest audience yields a different digest. |
| `item-owner` | The purchase date is released to the owner and the legitimate-interest tier stays withheld. |
| `item-at-cutoff` | At a cutoff before the second model revision and the batch, only the first revision is read, later sources and observations are excluded by name, and no batch fact is invented. |
| `item-tie-and-wrong-unit` | Two observations at the same instant that disagree are a conflict; an observation in another unit is no observation. |
| `item-missing-model` | Without a model source the model fields are unknown with a missing-source finding; nothing is fabricated and the derivation is partial. |
| `item-cycle` | A relationship that returns to the subject is a cycle finding; the derivation stays partial rather than wrong. |
| `model-subject` | The model at its current revision as the subject: item and batch fields are outside the subject, not missing facts. |

`packaging-v1.json` is generated by `packages/dpp-profiles/test/packaging-fixture.test.ts` in the same form and applies the same derivation to a packaging relationship graph under the published `general@2` profile, carried once under `shared.profile`: a demonstration 750 ml bottle under the 952 prefix as the container, its closure and label as components related to it by `component-of` and carrying their own material mass observations, the deposit return scheme as a related document on the model, the producer-responsibility registration as certification-shaped evidence on the item, and the filled product the bottle is itself a component of, related in the other direction. The container projection reads the container's own model and item sources and names the source of every value; the component relationships are recorded and not entered, so no component mass is folded into the container and no content fact is read from the bottle, and the content projected as its own subject reads only its own source. It demonstrates how scoped content, material and return information is related and projected; it is not a packaging regulatory profile, claims no packaging instrument's field list, and the profile it uses is the general one already published.

| Vector | What it proves |
|---|---|
| `container-public` | Model and item values each name their source revision; the closure and label relationships are recorded without being entered; the return scheme and the registration evidence are carried as documents; the content is not read. |
| `content-subject` | The filled product as its own subject reads only its own item source, records the bottle relationship, and reports its model fields unknown with a missing-source finding. |
| `container-without-model` | Without the bottle's model source the model fields are unknown with a missing-source finding and nothing is fabricated from the components. |

Refusals the tests exercise and the vectors do not carry, because they throw rather than derive: a non-finite number anywhere in a payload, and a record supplied at two revisions that no relationship pins.
