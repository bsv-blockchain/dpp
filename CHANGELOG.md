# Changelog

This file records what a consumer of the `@bsv/dpp-*` packages, the contracts and the fixtures needs: the changes in the unpublished candidate and the releases already published. Every package is an experimental beta prerelease whose API may change significantly. The standard is pre-1.0, so breaking changes are expected and recorded here without apology until version 1.0 is declared, as `GOVERNANCE.md` states. [Migration](docs/migration.md) gives the steps for each change that needs one.

## Unpublished: dpp-release-2026-11

`dpp-release-2026-11` is the current candidate: `@bsv/dpp-protocol` and `@bsv/dpp-profiles` `0.3.0-beta.10`, `@bsv/dpp-overlay` `0.4.0-beta.13`, `@bsv/dpp-credentials` `0.2.0-beta.6` and `@bsv/create-dpp-app` `0.1.0-beta.2`. Each line below is a change since the last publication, `dpp-release-2026-10-10` on 16 October 2026. A step number is a step of [test the November source candidate](docs/migration.md#test-the-november-source-candidate).

- `managed-custody@2` is the one custody profile: readers read `dpp-managed-acceptance@1` under its direct-signature rule and `@2` under the derived key, and nothing writes `@1` (step 1).
- `signManagedAcceptance` takes the state-signing `DataSigner` in place of the removed `AcceptanceSigner` interface and writes `dpp-managed-acceptance@2` only, signed by the custodian's BRC-42 child for `[1, 'dpp managed record v1']`; `MANAGED_CUSTODY_PROFILE` is `managed-custody@2`, which the index declares, and `MANAGED_RECORD_PROTOCOL_ID`, `managedRecordVerificationKey`, `MANAGED_ACCEPTANCE_FORMAT_V1` and `MANAGED_CUSTODY_PROFILE_V1` are new (step 2).
- Under `managed-custody@2` each state locks to its own field-6 controller key (`spec/managed-custody.md` §2).
- The owner-signed transfer, `owner-consent@1`, is removed: `checkOwnerConsent`, `OWNER_CONSENT_REFUSALS`, `OWNER_LINKAGE_KEY`, the `ownerConsent` option and `ownerConsentValid` are gone, `ownerConsentPolicy` leaves `@bsv/dpp-overlay/server`, the index drops the `consent-not-proven` refusal and the capability document's `ownerConsent` and `transferAuthorities`, and control authorities default to none (step 7).
- `normaliseTransferAuthorities` is `normaliseControlAuthorities`, the `ChainFailureKind` `consent` is `commitment` and `StateInspection.consentError` is `commitmentError` (step 7).
- The holder instruction record is parked outside the standard: a custodian's `UPDATE` or `RETIRE` for a managed holder after a committed `TRANSFER` depends on custody, and the report's limits say so (step 3).
- The index refuses to boot with `OWNER_CONSENT`, `TRANSFER_AUTHORITIES`, `INSTRUCTION_COMMITMENT` or `EXPORT_SIGNING_KEY` set: recovery keys go in `CONTROL_AUTHORITIES` and the export key in `EXPORTER_PRIVATE_KEY` (steps 3 and 9).
- Record version 3, the token carrier, is parked: the packages read and write versions 1 and 2 only, every version 3 export is gone, and the index drops the `carrier-prefix-invalid` and `token-id-mismatch` refusals and the `tokenId` lookup and declares no `dpp-record` version `3` (step 8).
- The verification report is version 2, without the reason codes `carrier-prefix-invalid`, `token-id-mismatch`, `consent-not-proven` and `resource-limit`; a version 1 report validates under `verification-report-v1.schema.json` (step 11).
- `verifyPassportEvidence` checks each token state's `payload_public` against its declared profile through the policy's `payloadValidator`, which `profilePayloadValidator(ajv)` from `@bsv/dpp-profiles` supplies; the new reasons are `profile-undeclared` and `schema-unavailable` (step 4).
- A native claim names its state by outpoint, `<txid>:<outputIndex>`, in `recordId` and carries no `evidence` property; `signLifecycleClaim` and the registry contract refuse anything else (step 4).
- Evidence packages are signed by the BRC-42 child of the exporter's identity key for `[1, 'dpp evidence package v1']` and the passport identifier: `EVIDENCE_PACKAGE_PROTOCOL`, `evidencePackageSigner` and `evidencePackageSigningKey` are new, the capability document names `exporterIdentityKey`, and `startOverlayService` takes `exportIdentityKey` (step 9).
- Acceptance records travel in evidence packages under `authority`, keyed by commitment, and `acceptanceRecordsInPackage` reads them back (step 3).
- The complete export and `GET /history` are reference features of `@bsv/dpp-overlay`, outside the overlay contract, and `evidence-export.schema.json` moved from `contracts/` and `@bsv/dpp-protocol/schemas/` to the overlay package's `schemas/` (step 9).
- `passport-source.schema.json` and `passport-projection.schema.json` moved to `@bsv/dpp-profiles/schemas/`; passport projections are a reference feature of that package, and `projectPassport` is unchanged (step 12).
- `@bsv/dpp-credentials` no longer carries the parked VSC profile, its Ed25519 and BBS suites or the `epcis-vsc@1` and `epcis-vsc@2` mappings, and `./epcis-source` loses `readEpcisSourceReference` and `EPCIS_MAPPING_PROFILES` (step 6).
- The overlay contract is `0.14.0-draft`: a core, `x-dpp-core-version` `1`, and versioned extensions, informative `X-Admission-Refusal` codes, and `403 token-publisher-mismatch` on the write routes (step 10).
- The registry contract is `0.6.0` and lists the seven operations every registry serves: the report, history, health, statistics, certification, profile, showcase, GS1 resolver, printed-label and status list routes and the legacy intake are gone, a `vsc-seal-json-v1` submission is refused as `unsupported` and a historical UORA body as `invalid` (step 10).
- The registry contract adds the `epcis-event-vc@1` envelopes, `application/vc+jwt`, `ES256K` and an external credential's own type in `UoraType`.
- `contracts/interoperability.yaml` is removed: EPCIS import routes are the reference application's own (step 10).
- `epcis-event-vc@1`, proposed: `@bsv/dpp-protocol` signs and verifies a GS1 EPCIS event as a W3C credential keyed by its CBV 2.0 Event Hash ID (`epcisEventHashId`, `buildEpcisEventCredential`, `signEpcisEventCredential`, `verifyEpcisEventCredential`, `epcisEventAnchorMetadata`, `epcisEventCredentialVerifierFor`, `epcisEventCredentialKey`), and the report gains `event-hash-mismatch`, `event-hash-unsupported` and `error-declaration-unsupported`.
- `@bsv/dpp-profiles` lists `epcis-event-vc@1` in `EXCHANGE_PROFILE_IDS` and the parked `epcis-vsc@2` in `INTEROPERABILITY_PROFILE_IDS` and ships both manifests; the exchange, interoperability and operator manifest schemas accept the status `parked`, and the interoperability schema gains an optional `crosswalk`.
- `@bsv/dpp-overlay` issues per-publisher write tokens (`PUBLISHER_TOKENS_FILE` or its MongoDB, `dpp-index tokens`), registers a publisher key on a wallet-signed `dpp-index-registration@1` request at `POST /register` (`REGISTRATION`, `dpp-index registrations`), and writes an application's connection file with `dpp-index connection` (step 5).
- `@bsv/dpp-protocol` signs and inspects the registration request (`signRegistration`, `inspectRegistration`), which `fixtures/index-registration-v1.json` pins, and `appendPublisherVersion` builds the policy version a registration appends (step 5).
- The historical anchor rail takes no new writes: `UoraAnchorTopicManager` admits an anchor only from a service `ANCHOR_SERVICE_KEYS` names, and nothing when it names none.
- SHIP/SLAP peer discovery and advertising stay options of `@bsv/dpp-overlay`, parked outside the standard.
- The Compose preset makes `SUBMIT_TOKEN` and `ARC_CALLBACK_TOKEN` optional, accepts `MONGO_URL` for a managed database and runs the index container as the `node` user, read-only.
- `@bsv/create-dpp-index` is retired: its published `0.1.0-beta.1` stays on npm, and a new index runs from the Compose preset (step 5).
- `@bsv/create-dpp-app` writes `.env` with fresh secrets and the testnet index at scaffold, signs claims with the outpoint `recordId`, adds `npm run go-live` and `npm run connect-index --from <file>`, and has `npm run wallet` generate `WALLET_ROOT_KEY` on its first run.
- The drafts `battery@3`, `textile@3` and `textile@4` are parked and still readable, and `battery@4` is the one draft; `federated-operators@1` and the parked exchange and interoperability manifests carry the status `parked`, which `checkSelection` names in a note (step 12).
- The host a GS1 Digital Link identifier is minted under SHOULD be a GS1-Conformant Resolver, and one that is not SHALL answer every `/01/` path with 307 to one (`spec/gs1-discovery.md` §1).
- `GS1_LINK_TYPES` gains `dpp`, `masterData` and `epcisRepository`, with `epcis` deprecated, and `resolutionSetProblems` checks a resolver's records as a set.
- A writer MUST NOT start a new lineage with a version 1 genesis, and SHOULD continue a version 1 lineage with the version 2 upgrade ([start writing version 2](docs/migration.md#start-writing-version-2)).
- A writer that announces before sending MUST withdraw a draft the network refused from every index it announced it to that serves the retraction extension (`spec/writing.md` §3).
- A disclosure of a restricted tier is re-encrypted to the reader's identity key and carries no field of a tier the reader is not entitled to (`spec/record-model.md` §7).
- The release selection requires one claim per role under `native-baseline@2`, `gs1-identifier-discipline` and `profile-governance`; `native-baseline@1` is frozen history, and the standard makes no European system conformance or product-compliance claim.
- Superseded release sets and their selections are under `release/archive/`, and publication receipts and approved plans under `docs/reference/publication-history/`.
- Fixtures moved: `attestation-anchor-v1.json` and its vector form are a claim about the genesis of `chain-v1.json`, so their digests, signature and script changed; `chain-v1.json` lost the seven owner-signed transfer refusals and `evidence-v1.json` its `consent-not-proven` case; the `TRANSFER` of `chain-v2.json` commits to a version 2 acceptance record; `record-v2.json` pins a version `3` output as the refusal `unsupportedVersion` again, formerly `versionThreeWithoutPrefix`; every evidence report, `battery-lifecycle-v1.json`'s included, reads `reportVersion` `2` without `ownerConsentValid`, and `profile-undeclared` where a payload declares no profile; the evidence package vectors are signed by the derived exporter key; and `managed-acceptance-v2.json` and `index-registration-v1.json` are new.
- The version 3 fixtures, `record-v3.json`, `chain-v3.json` and `evidence-v3.json` with their vectors, moved to `docs/reference/parked/token-carrier/` (step 8).
- New vectors: `exchange/epcis-event-vc/v1.json`, `interoperability/epcis/hash-v1.json` and the parked `interoperability/epcis/v2.json`; `interoperability/gs1/resolution.json` gains two walk-up and six set vectors and uses the link type `gs1:epcisRepository`.
- The vector-form copies of the record, chain, historical anchor and acceptance fixtures are removed from `fixtures/vectors/dpp/`: the top-level files are their one form, `fixtures/README.md` lists the test keys the copies published, and the four length-bound refusals only `anchor/v3.json` pinned are built by a test, as the guide describes. `attestation-anchor/v1.json` stays, because it pins the current anchor's six refusals.
